A closed-loop PID controller built in hardware with op-amps and power ICs, after identifying the plant in MATLAB. A second drive uses encoder feedback, a 3-phase rectifier and thyristor firing-angle control to hold speed under varying load.

Solar PV

Solar monitoring and panel characterization

An Arduino Nano system with INA219, BH1750 and DS18B20 sensors and an LCD, used to log the I-V and P-V curves of a 10 W panel and the effect of light, temperature, angle and load on its output. I also presented work on solar PV with battery storage at the 10th International Electrical Engineering Conference.
